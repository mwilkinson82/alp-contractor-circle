# ALP Drip Campaign Plan

## 4 Sequences

### Sequence 1: Estimating Checklist Single-Dippers
- 5 emails: Day 2, 4, 7, 10, 14
- Target: Downloaded estimating checklist only

### Sequence 2: Q1/Q2 Framework Single-Dippers
- 5 emails: Day 2, 4, 7, 10, 14
- Target: Downloaded Q1/Q2 framework only

### Sequence 3: Double-Dippers
- 3 emails: Day 0 (immediate), Day 3, Day 6
- Target: Downloaded 2+ lead magnets
- Note: 22 already emailed are excluded

### Sequence 4: Homepage-Only Signups
- 3 emails: Day 1, 3, 5
- Target: Opted in from main website without downloading a specific lead magnet

## Key Rules
- Double-dippers get pulled OUT of single-dipper sequences
- Double-dippers get their own shorter, more direct sequence
- All sequences nurture toward Contractor Circle ($497/mo) or 1-on-1 Session ($1,000)
- Existing CC members should be excluded from all drip campaigns
